# WarBreach

A landscape base-defense game for phones. Build and upgrade a military base, then survive endless waves of enemy armies.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or host the folder on any static host (GitHub Pages works). On a phone, use **Add to Home Screen** for a fullscreen landscape app. Progress saves in the browser automatically.

## Features

- Top-down military compound with fence, gates and approach roads
- Command Center (HQ levels 1–5) that unlocks buildings, storage and higher levels
- Economy: Gold Mine, Steel Foundry, Supply Depot (tap bubbles to collect)
- Defenses: Machine Gun Nest, Cannon, Mortar Pit, Sniper Tower, Missile Battery
- Walls in five tiers that link together as you lay them
- Build timers with 2 builders, upgrade / move / sell, offline production
- Touch controls: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, drag the outline to place
- Battle mode: endless waves come down the west, east and north roads
  - Riflemen, Grenadiers, Armored Jeeps, Light Tanks and Attack Drones
  - Enemies path around walls when they can and break through when they can't
  - Each defense fires its own way: MG tracers, cannon shells, lobbed mortars, sniper shots, homing rockets
  - The Command Center has a rooftop gun
  - Kills and cleared waves pay gold and steel; damage is repaired after the battle
  - Speed x1/x2/x3, retreat, best-wave record

- **Research Lab** (Clash of Clans / Boom Beach): permanent base-wide upgrades such as damage, hit points, production, airstrikes, build speed and repairs.
- **Daily challenge** (Bloons TD 6): a new five-wave challenge every day with two random rule changes and a medal reward.

- **Service record**: lifetime stats from the menu.

## Update 3.5: stability and fixes

Found with automated stress tests: battle soak runs at every Command Center level, threat, doctrine and War Games mix; thousands of random UI actions; two simulated weeks of base life; damaged and old saves.

- Titan Mech now arrives from wave 20 (wave 10 is a Siege Tank again) with slightly less armor; it could stall mid-level bases for minutes.
- Late endless waves are a little smaller (fewer Riot Troopers and Kamikaze Drones).
- Build queue: upgrading a queued building yourself removes the queued entry, so it is not upgraded a second time.
- Resources above the storage limit (after selling a Supply Depot) are never cut by refunds, mission, login, War Chest, Quartermaster, raid or outpost rewards.
- The Quartermaster refuses gold and steel crates when that storage is full instead of taking the medals.
- Outposts keep their income when storage is full; surprise-attack win reports never show negative loot.
- Upgrade cards never open over a defeat and close with the battle; pausing while choosing one keeps the battle on hold.
- Cargo drones stay home during battles; Collect all reports oil correctly.
- Damaged saves repair themselves (invalid resource values, a missing Command Center, unknown troops or items).
- Units under heavy fire keep their colors instead of flashing white; the Illumination Flare is less blinding; Shield Generators are cheaper to run.

## New-player intro and tutorial

A new player's first launch opens with a short intro: the camera sweeps in from the west road over the outpost, the title lands, and Sgt. Vance, the field advisor, gives a three-line briefing and asks you to name your outpost. Every part can be skipped.

Then Sgt. Vance runs an 18-step guided tutorial with a spotlight on what to tap: look around, collect gold, build a second Machine Gun Nest, upgrade the Command Center, meet the builders, start the first battle, move the hero, call an airstrike, clear the wave, learn about retreating, and find the Campaign. Each step finishes when you actually do it, and finishing pays 600 gold, 400 steel and 20 medals.

- The advisor card never covers what it points at; on small phones it moves beside the target.
- It never blocks the screen: you can do anything at any time. Starting a battle early jumps ahead to the battle steps; closing the shop, cancelling a placement or closing the briefing steps back; steps that cannot be done (no gun slots left, the Command Center already upgrading) are skipped; if you are short on resources, Command sends a supply drop.
- Skip it any time (two taps), and replay the tutorial or the intro from **Settings > Data**.
- Returning players never see it. Players who were part-way through the old four hints get the new tutorial without the intro.

## Update 5.6: bigger base and balance

Found by simulating endless waves at every Command Center level, all 18 campaign missions and full Gauntlet runs, plus random UI testing.

**Bigger base:** the build area grew from 28 x 18 (504 tiles) to 38 x 26 (988 tiles) on a 46 x 34 map. From Command Center 9 up, the buildings and walls you may own no longer fit in the old area (at Command Center 12 they need 745 tiles); now a full Command Center 12 base fits with about a quarter of the ground free for paths. Existing bases, obstacles, the supply crate and saved layouts move to the middle of the new ground automatically, and base codes made before 5.6 still import in the right place.

**Fixes:**
- Waves could stay open forever: enemies parked just outside the reach of every defense and shelled an outlying building that a Repair Bay kept healing (Artillery Trucks and Stealth Commandos did the same from range). At Command Center 6–8 most waves stalled this way. Now, once a wave has nothing left to send, 30 seconds with no kill and no building destroyed make the remaining attackers charge the Command Center, and anything still alive after 2 more minutes without progress pulls back.
- An Overtime Shift bought before closing the game now keeps doubling production for the part of it spent away.

**Balance:**
- The Gauntlet was a wall at its unlock level (a full Command Center 5 base lost at stage 3) and is now a climb to the Warlord. The first stage starts at enemy level 4 at Command Center 5, and each stage adds 1.5 levels. From Command Center 8 the boss stage stays where it was, so battles do not get longer.
- Campaign mission 11, Total War: enemy level 26 → 20 (mission 10 is level 11, mission 12 is level 34).
- Steel Foundry: 20 → 26 steel per minute (storage raised to match). Upgrading everything took 1.5–2.4x as long in steel as in gold.
- Oil Derrick: 8 → 12 oil per minute (storage raised to match). Levels 9+ took up to 2.2x as long in oil as in gold.

**Tests:** `node tests/logic.test.js` runs its checks in a headless browser (needs Playwright): stalled waves end, kills reset the timer, waves keep moving at Command Center 3, 6 and 9, offline boosts, Gauntlet scaling, storage versus upgrade costs, resource pacing, the campaign curve, every building fitting at every Command Center level, and moving old saves and base codes onto the bigger map.
